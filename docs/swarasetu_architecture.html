SwaraSetu End-to-End System Topology

1. Deterministic WHO IMCI Clinical Safety

100% auditable rule engine guaranteeing zero LLM hallucination in clinical calculations.

3. 4-Pillar Emergency Response & Edge Sync

Instant life-saving interventions for acute rural emergencies and offline resilience.

  • Hospital Readiness Alerts: Pre-alerts duty doctors to prepare Anti-Snake Venom (ASV) vials, ECG bays, or Magnesium Sulfate.
